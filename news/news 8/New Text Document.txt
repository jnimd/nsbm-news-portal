NSBM Green University proudly represented Sri Lanka at the 88th Executive Committee Meeting of the SAARC Chamber of Commerce and Industry (SAARC CCI), held on 16 November 2026 in the Maldives. The meeting brought together representatives from across South Asia to discuss important matters related to regional economic cooperation, sustainable development and opportunities for strengthening partnerships among institutions and organisations in the region.

The NSBM delegation consisted of Vice-Chancellor Professor E. A. Weerasinghe, Senior Deputy Vice-Chancellor Professor Chaminda Rathnayake and Head of Administration Mr. Kapila Liyanage. Their participation provided an opportunity for NSBM to engage with regional representatives and contribute to discussions focused on improving cooperation and building stronger connections across South Asian countries.
