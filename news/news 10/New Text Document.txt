NSBM Green University has taken another important step towards expanding international higher education opportunities in Sri Lanka by signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Southern Cross University (SCU), Australia. The agreement introduces a new double degree programme combining a Bachelor of Business and a Bachelor of Information Technology, which is expected to commence in 2027.

The agreement was formalised as part of NSBM Green University's 10th Anniversary Celebrations, marking a significant milestone in the longstanding academic partnership between the two universities. The MoU was signed by Professor Tyrone Carlin, Vice-Chancellor of Southern Cross University, and Professor E. A. Weerasinghe, Vice-Chancellor of NSBM Green University. Both institutions reaffirmed their shared commitment to providing internationally aligned education and preparing students for the changing demands of the global employment market.

The proposed double degree programme is designed to bring together two important fields: business and information technology. In today's rapidly developing digital world, organisations increasingly require professionals who understand both business operations and technological solutions. By combining these two academic disciplines, the programme aims to provide students with a broader understanding of how technology supports business development, management and innovation.

Students following this programme will have the opportunity to develop knowledge and skills in areas related to business management, information technology and digital innovation. This combination can help prepare graduates for a variety of career opportunities in the modern workplace, where business knowledge and technical expertise are becoming increasingly valuable.

Beyond the introduction of the double degree, the MoU represents the continued development of the relationship between NSBM and Southern Cross University. It also provides a foundation for further academic collaboration, research opportunities and international engagement between the institutions.

This partnership reflects NSBM Green University's ongoing commitment to broadening educational opportunities for Sri Lankan students through international collaborations. By bringing globally aligned academic programmes to Sri Lanka, the university aims to provide students with valuable learning experiences while supporting the development of skilled graduates who can contribute to both local and international industries.

As NSBM celebrates a decade of progress in higher education, this collaboration with Southern Cross University represents another step towards strengthening its international academic presence and preparing future graduates for an increasingly interconnected and technology-driven world.
