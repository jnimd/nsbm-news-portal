NSBM Economics and Decision Sciences Students Host ECONOMICA 2026 
The Students’ Circle of Economics and Decision Sciences (SCEDS) of the Faculty of Business at NSBM Green University successfully hosted ECONOMICA 2026, its flagship academic initiative, under the theme “Harnessing Sri Lanka’s Economic Resources.” The event brought together young economic thinkers, academics, and distinguished industry experts to explore the potential of Sri Lanka’s economic resources and their role in driving sustainable national development.
A key highlight was the official launch of NEXsurge, a magazine featuring thought-provoking articles and fresh perspectives on harnessing Sri Lanka’s economic resources for sustainable development. The publication provides a platform for sharing knowledge, encouraging critical thinking, and highlighting the perspectives of emerging economic thinkers.
The event also recognised outstanding contributions to the field through its inter-university and intra-university competitions. Sanithi Jayasinghe, representing the BA (Hons.) in Financial Economics programme at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, was named the Inter-University Winner, while Pramodi De Silva, from the BSc in Business Management (Project Management) (Special) programme at NSBM Green University, secured the Intra-University Winner title.
The competition entries were evaluated by a distinguished panel of external reviewers comprising Ms. Ganga Madhushani, Senior Lecturer at the University of Kelaniya; Dr. Amanda Herath, Senior Lecturer at the University of Colombo; and Dr. Dilrukshi Hewage, Lecturer in Accounting and Finance at Staffordshire University, UK.
Adding further depth to the programme, a panel discussion titled “From Potential to Progress: Harnessing Sri Lanka’s Resources for Sustainable Economic Growth” brought together leading voices in economics and policy. The discussion featured Dr. Nisha Arunatilake, Director of Research at the Institute of Policy Studies of Sri Lanka; Ms. Arani Rodrigo, Chief Economist and Head of the Economic Intelligence Unit at The Ceylon Chamber of Commerce; and Professor Janaka Fernando of the Department of Business Economics, University of Sri Jayewardenepura. The panel explored the opportunities, challenges, and strategies involved in effectively utilising the country’s economic resources to achieve sustainable growth.
ECONOMICA 2026 reflected SCEDS’s commitment to fostering economic awareness, nurturing young talent, and creating meaningful platforms for academic and professional exchange. By bringing together fresh perspectives and expert insights, the initiative highlighted the important role of young minds in transforming Sri Lanka’s economic potential into meaningful progress.
