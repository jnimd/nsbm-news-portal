NSBM Green University proudly celebrated the Nursing Pledge Ceremony 2026 on 11 November 2026, marking a significant milestone in the academic and professional journeys of students from the 25.2 and 25.3 Nursing batches. The ceremony provided an opportunity for nursing undergraduates to formally express their commitment to the nursing profession and its responsibilities towards individuals, families and communities.

The event was honoured by the presence of Mrs. H. M. C. M. Herath, Former Chief Nursing Officer and Lecturer in Nursing at the Faculty of Nursing, University of Colombo, who attended as the Chief Guest. The ceremony was also attended by several distinguished university representatives, including Professor E. A. Weerasinghe, Vice-Chancellor of NSBM; Professor Chaminda Rathnayake, Senior Deputy Vice-Chancellor; Professor Thilini de Silva, Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Academic); and Dr. Nuwanthi Katuwavila, Dean of the Faculty of Science.

Other academic representatives included Ms. Anne Pathirana, Dean of the Faculty of Business; Dr. Madhavi Hewadikaram, Dean of the Faculty of Postgraduate Studies and Professional Advancement and Head of the Department of Life Sciences; Ms. Gosia Betcha, representing the University of Plymouth, United Kingdom; Dr. Miruna Rabindrakumar, Head of the Department of Life Sciences; and Ms. Sandyani de Silva, Senior Grade Nursing Tutor and Head of the B.Sc. (Hons) Nursing Degree Programme.Nursing Tutor and Head of the B.Sc. (Hons) Nursing Degree Programme.

The ceremony also welcomed distinguished professionals from the healthcare sector, including nursing leaders from Sri Jayewardenepura General Hospital, Durdans Hospital and Shanthi Nivasa, together with senior nursing officers, nursing sisters and nursing officers. Parents of the nursing undergraduates were also present to share this memorable occasion and celebrate their children's achievements.

One of the highlights of the event was the ceremonial presentation of nursing caps to the students. The lighting of traditional three-wick oil lamps symbolised the knowledge, wisdom, compassion and dedication that are essential qualities of a professional nurse. These meaningful traditions represented the beginning of an important journey in which students will apply their academic knowledge and practical skills to provide quality healthcare and support those in need.

The Nursing Pledge Ceremony reflects NSBM's commitment to developing knowledgeable, responsible and compassionate healthcare professionals. As the students prepare to move forward in their nursing careers, the university extended its best wishes to both batches for a successful and meaningful professional journey.
