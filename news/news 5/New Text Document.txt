NSBM Psychology Undergraduates Exhibit Developmental Theories Cratively
Undergraduates of the 26.2 BSc (Hons) Psychology programme, following the University of Plymouth degree pathway at NSBM Green University, recently showcased their learning through a series of creative and insightful Exhibit Presentations.
As part of their academic activities, students explored the application of developmental psychology theories by examining how different aspects of human development are portrayed in films. Through this exercise, they connected theoretical concepts with real-world representations, critically evaluating how developmental theories are reflected in characters, behaviours and life experiences on screen.
Moving beyond conventional classroom learning, the activity provided students with an opportunity to demonstrate their understanding through creative exhibits while strengthening their critical thinking, analytical, teamwork and presentation skills. It also encouraged them to communicate complex psychological concepts in engaging and accessible ways.
Through their thoughtful analyses and creative presentations, the students demonstrated how academic knowledge can be translated into practical understanding. The initiative reflects NSBM’s commitment to providing an engaging and experiential learning environment that prepares future psychology professionals to apply their knowledge beyond the classroom
