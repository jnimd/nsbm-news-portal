NSBM Strengthens International Academic Ties with St. Joseph’s College, India
NSBM Green University has further expanded its international academic network through the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with St. Joseph’s College (Autonomous), Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India.
Initiated with the facilitation of CIMA Sri Lanka, this partnership marks another step in NSBM’s continued efforts to build meaningful global connections, foster academic collaboration, and expand its international engagement.
The occasion was attended by Mr. Sivasubramaniam Vithushan, Lead Manager – Business Development & Stakeholder Relationships; Mr. Tharindu Wijewardana, Country Head – Sri Lanka; and Dr. A. John Peter, Director, St. Joseph’s International, St. Joseph’s College, Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India.
Through this partnership, NSBM continues to expand its international connections and pursue opportunities for meaningful engagement with higher education institutions across the region
